## Option A — recommended

Getting a file from my phone to my laptop shouldn't be this annoying.

Android File Transfer is dead. AirDrop pretends Android doesn't exist. And every
other option wants me to upload my own photos to somebody else's server, wait,
then download them again.

So I built **Beam**.

Your phone and your laptop are already on the same Wi-Fi. They should just talk
to each other — and now they do.

→ Send files both ways over Wi-Fi, devices find each other on their own
→ Plug in a cable and browse your phone like a drive
→ Create folders, rename, move and delete phone files from your laptop
→ Drag files straight out of your phone into Finder
→ Works with iPhone too
→ No cloud, no account, no upload limits, nothing leaves your network

This is the first in a new series I'm starting: building real solutions to
problems I actually run into. The best projects start with "why is this still so
painful?" — and I hit a lot of those. More coming.

Free and open source. Try it:

## Two things to mention if people ask in the comments

- **Mac:** the app isn't code-signed yet, so the first launch needs
  right-click → Open.
- **Android:** your phone will ask permission to install from outside the Play
  Store — normal for any APK.
